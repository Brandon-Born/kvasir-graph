Microeconomics for Business

Kvasir Graph

An SVG price-and-quantity diagram editor that keeps economics geometry honest. Enter linear equations or exact coordinates instead of eyeballing lines in slide software, then copy the finished figure straight into your assignment.

Open editor → Subscripts (E₁, P_b) · Arrow-key nudge · Undo/redo

Equations in Q or P form
Plot expressions like Qd = 136 − 2P or P = 23 + Q/3. Double-click any curve to edit its equation and auto-fit the axes.
Snapped intersections & guides
Mark equilibrium pins point E and dashed axis projections to the crossing so everything follows when a curve shifts.
U-shaped cost curves & shading
Anchor ATC or AVC minimums onto MC, sketch freehand strokes, and click any bounded region to shade surplus or deadweight loss.
Word, PNG, SVG & PDF export
Copy high-DPI figures directly to the clipboard for Word or Google Docs, download vector SVG, or print landscape PDF.

Design notes

Plain static files with no accounts, telemetry, or build pipeline.

Opens straight from disk
Every page is plain HTML, CSS, SVG, and vanilla JavaScript. Clone the folder and open index.html or graph.html offline in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
Saved on this device
Edits persist in localStorage and sync live across open browser tabs. Download a JSON backup anytime to move workspaces between machines.
