Live Microeconomics for Business

Kvasir Graph

An interactive SVG economics diagram editor built for problem sets and exam review. Start from supply and demand equations or a blank grid, anchor U-shaped cost curves to marginal cost, mark exact equilibrium crossings with axis guides, and shade consumer surplus, producer surplus, or deadweight loss with a single click.

  • Equation plotting (Q or P form)
  • Auto equilibrium & axis guides
  • U-shaped cost curves (ATC / MC)
  • Click-to-shade bounded areas
  • Copy & shift curves
  • Word clipboard, PNG, SVG & PDF

Built for real assignments

Simple, dependable browser tools with no login walls or messy screenshots.

Precision from equations

Enter exact equations or coordinates instead of eyeballing lines in a slide deck. Intersections, dashed axis guides, and shaded regions stay mathematically anchored as you edit.

Ready for Word & PDF

Copy crisp diagrams directly to the clipboard for Word or Google Docs, export vector SVG or high-DPI PNG, or print cleanly to PDF without cropping screenshots.

Local-first & offline

Every tool runs directly in your browser with no backend, no account, and no internet required. Work saves automatically on your device, syncs across open tabs, and exports to JSON backups.
